      What you can do

      • This page shows the whole planet at one moment. It is not a forecast for where you live. For your own area, check your local air quality index.
      • Air quality indexes use a few colour-coded levels, each with a plain message about who is affected.5 When pollution rises above health-based limits, it is unhealthy first for sensitive groups, then for everyone as levels climb.
      • Sensitive groups include people with heart or lung disease, older adults, children and teenagers.6 On high-pollution days they are advised to cut back on long or hard outdoor exertion first.
      • Coughing or shortness of breath is a sign to take it easier.6 This page is not medical advice; talk to a health professional about your own situation.

      How AI was used in this project

      This project was built with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, directed by the project author. AI generated the application code, the sound design (every sound is synthesized live in your browser by that code), the scripts that turn NASA's frames into data, the text on this page and the logo icon.

      Not AI-generated: the air data and maps (NASA), the Earth surface image (Solar System Scope), borders and place names (Natural Earth), and the health guidance (WHO, US EPA). No AI image, video or audio generator was used, and no NASA logos or mission identifiers appear.
